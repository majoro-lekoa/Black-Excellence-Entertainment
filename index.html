Welcome to my official portfolio. I am Lekoa "Liquest" Majoro, a 22-year-old student at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology in Lesotho, pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Broadcasting and Journalism. Here you can explore my skills, creative work, projects, and how to get in touch with me.

About Me

I’m a media enthusiast, a storyteller, and a multi-disciplinary creative with a passion for voice, vision, and visuals. As a student of Broadcasting and Journalism, I’ve developed a deep understanding of the power of communication, and I’m constantly exploring new ways to use media as a force for expression and impact.

I’ve taken part in multiple creative projects, both in music and documentary production. I’m the co-founder of Black Excellent Entertainment, a record label focused on empowering youth voices in the creative industry. Through my passion for music, film, and storytelling, I aim to inspire others to pursue their creative paths unapologetically.

I believe in the potential of multimedia to tell real stories and provoke meaningful conversations, whether it’s through a camera lens, a microphone, or a journalistic report. With a blend of traditional media education and modern content creation, I’m building a career around authenticity, originality, and impact.
